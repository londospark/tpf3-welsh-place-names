## Croeso i Gymru! 🏴󠁧󠁢󠁷󠁬󠁳󠁿

Adds a **Welsh** option to the **Names** box when you start a new game, so your
towns, streets and residents are all named the Welsh way.

### What you get

**🏘️ Towns: 628 real Welsh places**
- The cities: Cardiff, Swansea, Newport, Wrexham, Bangor, St Davids
- Valley towns, market towns, seaside resorts and small villages from
  Holyhead to Chepstow, and St Davids to Prestatyn
- The real towns and villages along the **Talyllyn**, **Corris**,
  **Ffestiniog** and **Welsh Highland** heritage railways: Abergynolwyn,
  Pendre, Dolgoch, Corris, Tan-y-Bwlch, Tanygrisiau, Rhyd Ddu...
- And yes, **Llanfairpwllgwyngyllgogerychwyrndrobwllllantysiliogogogoch**.
  Good luck fitting a station sign.

**🛣️ Streets: 204 proper Welsh street names**
Stryd Fawr, Heol y Bont, Ffordd y Môr, Lôn Isaf, Maes-y-Dref, Teras y Capel,
Glan-yr-Afon, Rhodfa'r Môr... in place of the English pool.

**🧑‍🤝‍🧑 Residents: Welsh names**
230 Welsh first names (Gareth, Dafydd, Gethin, Iolo, Taliesin, Rhiannon,
Angharad, Myfanwy, Seren...) with 90 surnames common in Wales (Jones,
Davies, Pritchard, Bowen, Vaughan, Llewellyn...). Expect plenty of Joneses.

### How to use
1. Activate **Welsh Place Names** in the mod manager.
2. Start a **New Game**, and pick **Welsh** in the **Names** box.

Name sets are chosen when a map is created, so existing saves keep their
current names.

### Compatibility
Cosmetic only. Nothing runs during play, so it's safe to add or remove and
works with any other mod.
